04 / Make it physical

Two-color Jacquard · rectangular panel

Gauge values are placeholders until measured on a washed/relaxed Jacquard swatch. Width is limited to 252 needles.

PNG uses exact black and white opaque pixels (no texture, labels, anti-aliasing or scaling). JSON contains personal data and annotations: handle privately.

05 / Knit workflow

  1. Open Customist Studio.
  2. Create a rectangle with matching needle width. Use Birdseye Jacquard and import the PNG at the desired starting row.
  3. Assign black and white to two distinct yarn carriers; verify row direction and panel dimensions.
  4. Configure backing, edge closure, waste/cast-on and bind-off with the machine operator.
  5. Run error checks, knit a small test swatch and revise gauge before full export to K-code.
Import format and privacy

CSV or TSV with timestamp and numeric glucose. ISO dates with offsets are converted to your local clock. Ambiguous date formats may require reformatting to ISO. One or multiple samples per 5-minute bin are averaged. Missing bins remain visible. Files are processed locally; no server or analytics.

Research prototype inspired by Wear Your Heart on Your Sleeve (DIS 2023) and Stitching Meaning (TVCG 2026). Not a certified knitting compiler.
